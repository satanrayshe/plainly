Plainly in 90 seconds

Six links, in order. The samples open instantly because their results were prepared in advance. Step 4 is a live check: your browser reads a photo and only the words are checked.

The tour

  1. A scam that looks official 15 s

    An electricity "final notice" demanding payment to a personal UPI ID tonight. Look for the red verdict, each warning sign quoted from the letter, and where to report it.

    Open the electricity notice

  2. A notice in the genuine format 15 s

    An IRS balance-due notice whose contacts match the official list. The verdict still sends you to the official number. The deadline is worked out in code from the letter's own date (on a live check, "Add to calendar" saves any date still ahead). Then switch the explanation to हिन्दी at the bottom.

    Open the IRS notice

  3. A letter that talks to the AI 10 s

    This letter hides instructions meant to sway an AI checker. Plainly treats that as a strong scam sign. The instruction is visible only inside the image; the result never repeats it.

    Open the hidden-instruction letter

  4. A photo, read on your device 20 s

    Save the IRS sample image (or use any photo of a letter), then choose it on Check a letter. A progress bar shows Tesseract reading it inside the browser; the photo is never uploaded. The words appear in a box to correct, and only that text goes to the rules engine on AWS Lambda.

    Open Check a letter

  5. The receipts 10 s

    On any result, open "Receipts: every check we ran" to see each step marked FLAG, PASS or UNKNOWN with its timing. On a live check the first line is the reading done on your device. The rules and the verdict logic are listed in full on How it works.

    See the rules and the verdict logic

  6. The build agent's receipts 10 s

    The coding agent was connected to AWS through the AWS MCP Server under a least-privilege policy: no deletes through MCP, and no way to stop or reconfigure the audit trail once it was running. CloudTrail shows what it did.

    Open the evidence
